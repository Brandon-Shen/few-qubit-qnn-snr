Scope: 4-qubit open-boundary TFIM, nominal depths [1, 2, 3, 4, 6], finite-shot budgets [250, 500, 1000, 2000], 50 matched initializations, 30 replicates per finite-shot cell. Conclusions below are limited to this tested design; they are not general claims about barren plateaus, other qubit counts, or other circuit families.

## Confirmatory results (H1-H4, Holm-adjusted, family-wise alpha=0.05)

- **H1** (eta_EL, exact-gradient signal scale): H0 is **rejected** after Holm correction (estimate=0.004346, p_holm=0.01345). Rejected H0 (two-sided); bootstrap CI direction not conclusive.
- **H2** (beta_EL, estimator-SNR scale): H0 is **rejected** after Holm correction (estimate=0.02373, p_holm=0.0001066). Rejected H0 (two-sided); bootstrap CI direction not conclusive.
- **H3** (beta_ER, estimator-SNR scale): H0 is **not rejected** after Holm correction (estimate=0.003528, p_holm=1). Failed to reject H0 after Holm correction; this is not evidence of additivity, equivalence, or that the interventions have identical effects.
- **H4** (beta_LRd, estimator-SNR scale): H0 is **not rejected** after Holm correction (estimate=0.0005109, p_holm=1). Failed to reject H0 after Holm correction; this is not evidence of additivity, equivalence, or that the interventions have identical effects.

H1 tests an interaction in the **exact-gradient signal** (asinh(|exact_gradient|)); H2-H4 test interactions in the **estimator SNR** (asinh(SNR_est)). These are different outcome scales and different scientific claims -- a rejection in one family does not imply a rejection in the other, and neither is a claim about optimizer or task success on its own.

Any hypothesis marked 'not rejected' above is **not** evidence that the tested coefficient equals zero, that the two interventions are additive, or that they have identical effects; no equivalence test was prespecified for this run.
