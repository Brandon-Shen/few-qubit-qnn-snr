# Confirmatory H2–H4 numbers: end-to-end-only adopted, pooled numbers superseded

**This document supersedes the pooled-mode confirmatory numbers previously
reported in `results/production_confirmatory/confirmatory_hypotheses.csv` /
`results/production_confirmatory/holm_adjustment.csv` / `results/production_confirmatory/snr_model_coefficients.csv`.** The
pooled fit mixed `finite_shot_conditional` and `finite_shot_end_to_end` rows
into a single model (`verification/mode_pooling_guard.md` explains the bug
and its fix; `verification/conditional_vs_endtoend_comparison.md` is the
full derivation of the end-to-end-only numbers adopted here). The paper's
Methods section has always designated end-to-end as the confirmatory
estimator and conditional as diagnostic-only — this adoption brings the code
and the official record in line with that, it does not change the paper's
design.

## What is now the official record

`results/production_confirmatory/confirmatory_hypotheses.csv`, `results/production_confirmatory/holm_adjustment.csv`, and
`results/production_confirmatory/snr_model_coefficients.csv` were regenerated by rerunning
`python -m qnn_snr fit --config configs/confirmatory.yaml` through the
now-guarded `cmd_fit` path (`verification/mode_pooling_guard.md`), whose
default `--mode` is `finite_shot_end_to_end`. This was a **fresh run through
the production code path**, not a copy-paste of the numbers already derived
by hand in `conditional_vs_endtoend_comparison.md` — and it reproduced them
exactly (bit-for-bit on every coefficient, SE, and p-value), confirming the
guard changes *which* data gets fit, not *how* it gets fit.

| Hypothesis | Coefficient | Estimate | SE | Wald z | p (unadjusted) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| H1 | `eta_EL` (`E:L`, exact-signal model) | 0.004346 | 0.001529 | 2.842 | 0.004482 |
| H2 | `beta_EL` (`E:L`) | 0.024996 | 0.007279 | 3.434 | 0.000595 |
| H3 | `beta_ER` (`E:R`) | -0.000958 | 0.007293 | -0.131 | 0.895536 |
| H4 | `beta_LRd` (`L:R:depth_z`) | -0.010179 | 0.005362 | -1.898 | 0.057658 |

H1's numbers are **unchanged** from the previous (pooled) record — H1 uses
`exact_df` (`analysis_mode == "statevector_exact"`), which has no finite-shot
mode at all and was never affected by the pooling bug.

## Holm–Bonferroni adjustment, recomputed (not assumed)

Ran `qnn_snr.stats.holm.holm_bonferroni` on the four end-to-end-only raw
p-values above (`[0.004482168916526863, 0.0005948289235313542,
0.8955359586892191, 0.05765827411115354]` for H1–H4 respectively), exactly
as `cmd_fit` does — this is the actual `results/production_confirmatory/holm_adjustment.csv` output,
not a manual recomputation:

| Hypothesis | p_unadjusted | p_holm | reject_after_holm |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| H1 | 0.004482 | 0.013447 | **True** |
| H2 | 0.000595 | 0.002379 | **True** |
| H3 | 0.895536 | 0.895536 | False |
| H4 | 0.057658 | 0.115317 | False |

**The reject/non-reject pattern is unchanged from the old pooled adjustment
(H1, H2 rejected; H3, H4 not) — but the adjusted p-values themselves are
not identical to the pooled ones**, since H2–H4's underlying raw p-values
changed when the fit switched from pooled to end-to-end-only data (compare
against `results/superseded_pooled/holm_adjustment_pooled_SUPERSEDED.csv`: old
`p_holm` was H1=0.013447 [same, H1 unaffected], H2=0.00010661 [pooled] vs
0.0023793 [end-to-end-only] — H2's adjusted p-value is about 22x larger
under end-to-end-only, though still comfortably below α=0.05; H3=1.0 in
both; H4=1.0 [pooled] vs 0.115317 [end-to-end-only] — H4 moved much closer
to the rejection boundary under end-to-end-only, though it still does not
cross it). **H4 in particular is worth flagging going forward**: at
p_holm=0.115, it is the closest to significance of the three non-rejected
hypotheses under the new adopted numbers, versus having been essentially
at the ceiling (p_holm=1.0) under the old pooled fit — this is a direct
consequence of the sign-and-magnitude split between modes documented in
`conditional_vs_endtoend_comparison.md` (conditional +0.0104, end-to-end
-0.0102), not a new analysis choice made here.

## What is not affected, and was not touched

Per the standing scope for this pass: H1 (see above — no finite-shot mode
input), the R_rep/N_init pilot-precision calibration findings (calibration
happens within a single mode/cell before any pooling could occur), the
entanglement diagnostics (computed from the final statevector, independent
of gradient-estimation mode), and the H1 finite-difference verification.
None of these files were opened or modified as part of this task.
