## Task 2 -- zero-variance exclusion audit

**Headline finding (bigger than the prompt anticipated)**: zero-variance
exclusion is **exactly, deterministically confined to `L=0`** across the
*entire* 204,800-cell dataset, both analysis modes, every depth and
budget -- 0 of 102,400 `L=1` cells flagged in either mode, with no
exceptions. D=1 has the highest *rate* of this L=0-only phenomenon (2.58%
end-to-end) but does not introduce a new failure mode absent elsewhere
(D=2 through D=6 show the same pattern at 0.27-0.66%). An exploratory
`zero_variance_excluded ~ E*L*R + log2(B)` logistic model was attempted and
correctly abandoned (complete quasi-separation on `L`, per the prompt's own
"do not force a model" instruction) in favor of the descriptive tables.
**Flagged**, since `L` is one of the two factors in the paper's headline
`beta_EL` interaction. Full detail:
`verification/zero_variance_exclusion_audit.md`.

## Task 3 -- mixed-model health and robustness diagnostics

- **A. Convergence/singularity**: adopted fit converges, `singular_fit=False`,
  one pre-existing (not new) `ConvergenceWarning` (MLE near a parameter-space
  boundary).
- **B. Independent optimizer check**: `bfgs` vs. `lbfgs` agree to 8-9
  significant figures on every target coefficient and 5 figures on the
  log-likelihood and random-effect variances -- no optimizer sensitivity.
- **C. Residual diagnostics**: real, systematic heteroscedasticity by block
  count (SD 0.659 at D=1 down to 0.276 at D=6) and by budget (SD 0.301 at
  B=250 up to 0.513 at B=2000, opposite direction), plus moderately
  heavy tails (1.14% of standardized residuals exceed \|3\|, ~4x the normal
  expectation). Reported as a genuine caveat on Wald SEs (likely mild
  underestimate at low D / high B), not a reason to discard the fit.
- **D. Leave-one-initialization-out (50/50 converged, 0 failures)**: no sign
  reversal for `beta_EL` or `beta_LRd`; `beta_EL` stays `p<0.05` in all 50
  deletions (H2's rejection is not attributable to any single
  initialization); `beta_ER` shows 16/50 sign flips, never near
  significance (consistent with a clean null); `beta_LRd` shows 18/50
  (36%) raw-p crossings of 0.05, the single most fragile result of the
  three by this metric -- a new, independent line of evidence pointing the
  same direction as every other H4 robustness check already in the paper.
  `beta_EL`'s single largest deletion (init 1) shifts the estimate by 1.131
  original-SE units -- flagged alongside Task 1's similar-magnitude
  finding, though neither of the two LOO-specific stop conditions (sign
  reversal; H2 flipping from clearly supported to unsupported) is
  triggered. Full detail: `verification/end_to_end_model_diagnostics.md`.

## Task 4 -- extended end-to-end bootstrap

**Final achieved n=443** (target: minimum 400, preferred 1000 -- 443 clears
the minimum, falls short of the preferred target, reported exactly as
achieved). **0 of 443 fits failed (0.0%)** across all four pooled streams.

| Coefficient | Wald estimate (95% CI) | Holm decision | Bootstrap median (n=443) | Bootstrap 95% CI (n=443) | Includes zero |
|---|---|---|---:|---|:-:|
| `beta_EL` (H2) | 0.024996 [0.010729, 0.039263] | **Rejected** (p_Holm=0.00238) | 0.023685 | [-0.018024, 0.065688] | Yes |
| `beta_ER` (H3) | -0.000958 [-0.015252, 0.013337] | Not rejected (p_Holm=0.896) | -0.001868 | [-0.030298, 0.024478] | Yes |
| `beta_LRd` (H4) | -0.010179 [-0.020688, 0.000331] | Not rejected (p_Holm=0.115) | -0.010811 | [-0.026975, 0.006403] | Yes |

**H2**: the confirmatory Wald/Holm rejection stands unchanged. The
percentile bootstrap **does not corroborate it** at n=443, and -- unlike
at every smaller n reported previously -- this is now stated as a stable
property of the resampling distribution rather than a small-sample
artifact, since the interval neither narrowed nor moved away from zero
across a 10x increase in iterations (n=40 to n=443; width actually grew
slightly, 0.068 -> 0.084). **H3**: clean null, bootstrap consistent
throughout. **H4**: genuinely inconclusive at every n tried, including the
final one; the interval's *shape* stabilized earliest of the three
coefficients but still straddles zero, consistent with (not resolving) the
paper's existing framing of H4 as fragile. No material-change stop
condition was triggered (bootstrap did not flip from including to
excluding zero; failure rate 0% <<1%). Full detail:
`verification/bootstrap_end_to_end_extended.md`.

## Unresolved limitations (carried forward, not resolved by this package)

- `beta_EL`'s D≠1 and single-initialization-deletion shifts (both ~1.0-1.1
  original-SE units) are flagged but not mechanistically explained beyond
  the circumstantial evidence that `D=1` and outlier initializations are
  both noisier subpopulations, not that they are driving a spurious effect.
- The `L=0`-only zero-variance pattern's mechanism (why the local-cost
  estimator apparently never produces an exactly-zero-variance replicate
  set) is not investigated here -- only documented as exact and universal.
- Bootstrap n=443 falls short of the QMI/QIP-preferred n=1000; reaching
  1000 was not attempted further after 443 comfortably cleared the stated
  minimum of 400, per the resource-ceiling decision made earlier in this
  conversation.
- Residual heteroscedasticity by block count and budget means reported
  Wald SEs are an approximation; a heteroscedasticity-robust or
  variance-modeling refinement was not attempted (out of scope for this
  package).
