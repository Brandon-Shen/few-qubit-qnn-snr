## Architecture, CNOTs and residual

`qnn_snr/gradients.py:77-107` resets a four-qubit state in every block. Each block applies four additive Ry angles and four ordered CNOTs, then returns four Z expectations except at the terminal objective. Classical transitions use 4-by-4 W and length-four b, without additional clipping, normalization, wrapping, activation or feature rescaling. Quantum parameter count is 4D; classical entries total 20(D−1). Classical derivatives are not generated by the inspected experimental row loop. The coherent helper QNode in `circuits.py` is not the production reset-block path.

`config.py:33-35`, `entangling_pairs`, and `circuits.py:55-88` specify these literal schedules:

| Schedule | Ordered CNOTs |
|---|---|
| Baseline, every block | 0→1, 1→2, 2→3, 3→0 |
| Restricted, odd blocks | 0→1, 2→3, 1→0, 3→2 |
| Restricted, even blocks | 1→2, 3→0, 2→1, 0→3 |

Independent scalar basis-amplitude gate construction matched each distinct schedule at one nontrivial angle vector. Twenty exact-forward cases corroborated reset/shape/terminal structure; these are bounded tests, not exhaustive validation of all archived states. No transpiler appears in this path. Restricted pairings within a block do not imply universally lower entropy or coherent inter-block transfer.

`residual.py:61-64` uses x(ell+1)=W(ell)z(ell)+b(ell)+gamma z(ell−1), with z(0)=0 and gamma=R. Eight matched schedule/objective/depth comparisons at D=1,2 had bit-identical costs, terminal states and complete quantum gradient vectors across R. Four D=3 cases preserved x(2) and changed x(3). The shortcut first acts at block three and has no added trainable parameter or gate. Document locations: main131–190, supplement60–68. See [stage 2](STAGE_2_ARCHITECTURE_OBJECTIVES_INITIALIZATION.md).

## Exact gradients

`gradients.py:115-160` uses ±π/2 parameter shifts for feature Jacobians and terminal objective derivatives, then reverse assembly including the two-block residual derivative. Additive x/theta entry gives matching local angle derivatives. Local parameter-shift correctness was not assumed sufficient for the complete hybrid chain rule.

An independent cost evaluator used its own gates, CNOT indexing, feature signs, classical recurrence and TFIM. Across 64 cases (two deterministic parameter draws; D=1,2,3,6; both schedules/objectives/residual levels), all 768 quantum derivatives passed the mixed criterion |error|≤2e−8+2e−5|FD| at both central steps, giving 1,536 comparisons. Maximum absolute errors were 2.536e−9 at 1e−4 and 2.702e−11 at 1e−5; there were no sign reversals. Near-zero relative errors were reported separately rather than hidden. These new checks are distinct from the historical 224-derivative manuscript validation. ## Finite-shot estimators, SNR, bias and signs

`gradients.py:204-303` samples terminal shifted measurements in both modes. Conditional mode retains exact nonterminal features; end-to-end mode samples and actually re-encodes them. Thirty-two bounded vector replicates at D=3, all eight configurations, two modes and two seeds verified mode behavior, angle/transition equations and exact B=250 sampling totals. Shift arms use separate draws; a measurement setting shares shots across its measured features. Components within a gradient vector therefore share noise.

`stats/pointwise.py:44-79` computes |sample mean|/sample SD with ddof=1, without sqrt(30). Signed bias is mean−exact; absolute bias is |mean−exact|, not mean absolute individual-replicate error. Six controlled cells verified these definitions and zero rules. Zero variance yields infinity for nonzero mean or NaN for zero mean; nonfinite SNRs are excluded by `stats/models.py:159-177`. Zero mean with positive variance remains eligible at SNR=0. Accepted H2–H4 entry points explicitly select end-to-end; frozen primary filtering was independently checked in stage 4.

**IA-002 (Medium):** the empirical sign statistic in `stats/pointwise.py:63` compares sign(sample mean) with sign(exact gradient), returning false if either is zero. Main255–261 leaves g unspecified in the displayed equation; supplement77 distinguishes empirical/population targets but omits the zero rule. Synthetic counterexamples distinguish mean-sign agreement from replicate-sign fractions. The formula should explicitly use the sample mean and state the zero convention. Earlier stage CSV line74 citations were corrected to line63 in the integrated register.

## Shot accounting

`budget.py:37-81` allocates floor(B/jobs), then gives one extra shot to the first remainder jobs in sorted order. B covers one complete gradient-vector replicate, including shared measurements. Independent four-qubit job totals are conditional/global 8D, conditional/local 8D+8, end-to-end/global 9D−1, end-to-end/local 9D+7. All 320 resource-table rows matched counts/allocation and conserved B∈{250,500,1000,2000}; minimum job allocation was four shots. The executed 32-vector check matched runtime calls/totals. Archived production resource summaries and zero-variance joins also agree. Millions of raw production shot columns were not independently reaggregated.

## Initialization and random streams

`replicate.py:108-122,146-169` draws quantum/classical arrays outside the eight-configuration loop using root/initialization/depth-specific namespaces. Quantum angles are uniform [0,2π); `residual.py:35-52` supplies Glorot-uniform W and zero b. All 250 initialization/depth shape/bound checks passed; all 500 quantum/classical initialization seeds were distinct, and small real orchestration checks confirmed matching seed identities. Different depths do not reuse shallow prefixes. Numeric stream labels in metadata are distinct from the literal namespace strings used by generation.

**IA-001 (Medium):** `seeds.py:17` reduces SHA-256 to 31 bits. Repository and independent hashlib-only censuses both found 480,000 shot-vector requests, 479,930 seeds and 70 duplicate assignments, none within a scientific cell. This establishes stream reuse, not identical gradients or a quantified inferential effect. Do not silently change frozen seed derivation; disclose and assess sensitivity separately.

Initialization-pilot independence is also unestablished (IA-007); the separately seeded replicate pilot must not be conflated with it. See [stage 6C](STAGE_6C_PILOTS_HISTORICAL_DIAGNOSTICS.md).
